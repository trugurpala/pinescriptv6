# Verified Signal

Verified Signal treats proof as a visible structure. Space separates claim from evidence, while measured lines show how one becomes the other. The composition feels calm because every element has a reason to exist. Nothing decorates an empty idea.

Deep ink creates a quiet field. Ice carries the main message, Pine blue marks action, signal green marks trustworthy progress, and evidence amber marks a boundary that needs attention. Color never works alone: labels, sequence numbers, and geometry repeat the same meaning. Every contrast and alignment is calibrated with painstaking care.

Scale follows the reader's decision. One human sentence leads. Supporting facts appear as compact signals, not walls of copy. Repeated cards establish rhythm, while generous negative space lets the sequence remain clear at both full size and GitHub width. The result should feel meticulously crafted by an expert who removed more than they added.

Typography behaves like evidence. Space Grotesk gives the central idea a distinct voice. IBM Plex Sans explains it plainly. IBM Plex Mono labels sources, states, and machine-readable steps. Words stay sparse and exact, integrated into the composition rather than laid over it.

The visual reference is a signal path: a request enters, context narrows it, evidence supports it, and an answer leaves with a visible limit. Fine grid marks and numbered checkpoints reward close inspection without becoming noise. Master-level execution lives in consistent spacing, crisp exports, balanced density, and the absence of accidental detail.
